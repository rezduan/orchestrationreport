## 2. Findings

### R28-01 — LOW — UR-23 option (c) and R27-05's list of calls describe an ordering that cannot be satisfied for the §6.2 parses

**Where:** §2.3 UR-23 "(c) … confining EV's unordered calls to EV's slot (after the §6.2 record pids, before PR, internal order still unspecified)". The same wording appears in §1.7.3 "Repeated calls" ("Confining them to EV's slot, after the §6.2 record pids and before PR, is an owner option under UR-23"), in §11.122 and in (yy). The R28Δ condition lists "(§6.2 parse, §6.3 lineage, §6.5 comparison, CR1/CR2 test)" as calls that may be issued after DG.

**Evidence:**
- §6.2: "e = pid(parent0(Q), Q) if np(Q) ≤ 1, else pid(Q^1, Q)".
- np and parent0 come only from "a strict `cat-file commit X` parse" (§1.1).
- Every revision operand is written as a full 40-hex oid ("Argv identity").
- The allowlist gives no other way to obtain parents.

**Counterexample:**
- Present C (ancestry merge), with one non-merge record Q in `rev-list T --not I.tip`.
- No pre-FINALIZE computation parses Q, because IA2 does not run for a present C. So the record pid's DIFF argv `<parent0(Q)> <Q>` cannot be formed until `cat-file commit <Q>` has been issued.
- Option (c) requires that parse to come after the record pids. No conforming implementation can do that.
- For the same reason a §6.2 parse can never be issued after DG. A repeat would be answered from the cache. So the R28Δ list over-includes it.
- The Revision 27 review's own suggestion said "after the §6.1 pids, before PR", which is feasible. Revision 28 changed it to "after the §6.2 record pids".

**Impact:**
- No outcome effect, and nothing is adopted.
- But an owner who adopted (c) as written would adopt a constraint that contradicts §6.2. The claimed effect ("would make the last call of a FINALIZE run determinate") is right only under a feasible wording.

**Smallest correction:** reword (c), in all four places, as "EV's unordered calls are issued within EV's slot, after the §6.2 enumeration (each §6.2 parse before the pid that needs it) and before PR, internal order otherwise unspecified". Optionally, drop "§6.2 parse" from the list of calls that can be issued after DG.

**Impact:**

- But these are presence/absence justifications in the current text that do not follow from the states, which is what §13 item 1 targets. R27-04 was fixed in one of the three places that use the pattern.

**Smallest correction:**
