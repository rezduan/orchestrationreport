### R14-04 — LOW — IA7's "Q can differ between candidates" is false; residual (l) can be closed (§1.7.3 IA7 paragraph, §11.32(l), §4 mapping note)

**Evidence.**
- §1.7.3: "Q can differ between candidates (landing-textual reads Bu)."
- §4: candidates "have … the same Tb".

**Argument.** Candidates in Cmin share pos(M), hence the same M, S, Tb and Tip, and they share X (End).

Landing-textual, r and e are therefore identical, and so is Q.

AN can differ only through Ht.

**Correction.** Replace the sentence with this argument and close residual (l). Per-candidate AN is still needed, because Ht differs.
